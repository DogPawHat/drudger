## Company Overview
- **What:** AI platform for the **AEC industry** (Architecture, Engineering, Construction)
- **Product:** Automates project admin — RFIs & submittals, email filing, field notes, meeting minutes, reporting
- **Customers:** World-leading architecture and engineering firms (adopted at scale)
- **Founded:** 2021
- **Headquarters:** Berlin, Germany
- **Website:** https://cogram.com

## Product Details
Cogram's AI agents automate:
- **Email Management** — AI filing and search
- **RFIs & Submittals** — Logging/review integrated with Procore
- **Meeting Minutes** — Automated in virtual/in-person meetings
- **Field Reports** — Voice observations + photos → automated reports

Integrates with: Procore, Deltek, Autodesk Construction Cloud, Microsoft Teams, Outlook, Zoom, Google Meet
